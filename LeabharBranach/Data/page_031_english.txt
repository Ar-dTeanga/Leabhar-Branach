What has happened to the alliance of good deeds and learning,
since the patron of the learned has not been spared from blame.
In his absence, the turning of fortune has brought sorrow,
since the passing into the province left no trace of beauty. For
the sake of [those] who sought the hospitality of the household. And
the assembly of his careful gathering, their lands and possessions
alongside them, left the territories to the north.
Where the finding of the law and customs was a shield for all,
through the noble ancestry of Kellagh of the gentle manner,
of the Domhnall of the pleasant countenance, from the north of the fair land,
of the castle of the soldiers, the province of the clergy of Glenmalure
indeed. The great court and its gathering of the first chief after
the slaying of the heroes — but the return of the warriors to the
place of Mugh Laish and the territory of Bhomhne, a fair portion from
the fortress of Fiubh. They sent their province's tribute in full submission.
They went to the chieftains to assemble a hosting of great praise,
proceeding with force and resolution through the ford for the good of all,
the resolve of the faithful warriors in their unity of purpose.
At the ancient place of the lake of permission. From the height with
counsel they brought advice of wisdom, of the teaching of truth,
at the ford of the lord — and the great lady of noble descent
and the taking of the fortress of tribute and the castle's freedom.
They sent their leaders to the assembly of Domhnachera. A
foundation of good fortune for Thuiphmheincallimh of Muimhin the son of
the hand. It is a greater loss, darkly, when the permission of a
nobleman falls, for the sake of the clan of Dhnabhois
Fibh, and the most noble of the province of the faithful in
great harmony. They assembled their bounty of gift and heritage,
their learning. They stood in their beauty, and together their
tribute and their people's governance was held.
Thus spoke the assembly of the learned.
