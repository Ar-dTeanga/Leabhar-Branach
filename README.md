## About

The Leabhar Branach (Book of the O'Byrnes) is an Early Modern Irish poetry anthology compiled in the early 17th century. It contains poems in praise of the O'Byrne family, who ruled Gabhal Raghnaill in what is now County Wicklow. The poems, composed between approximately 1550 and 1630, were written primarily by poets of the McKeogh (Mac Eochaidh) family. In 1622, Brian mac Phelim O'Byrne likely commissioned Brian Mac Giolla Phadraig to transcribe them into a single volume.

The original manuscript is lost. Two copies survive: one held by Trinity College Dublin (MS1288), and another at the Houghton Library, Harvard University. A print edition was published by Sean Mac Airt in 1944.

For more information, see the [Wikipedia article on Leabhar Branach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leabhar_Branach).
